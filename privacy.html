Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

After The Curve stats is a private tool with one user: the owner of the YouTube channel After The Curve.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics data through Google's APIs with read-only access: views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention and subscriber counts. These are totals for the channel and its videos. The tool does not read information about individual viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to produce a weekly report for the channel owner. The report and the sign-in token are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred to anyone else.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored reports from their computer.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the About page of the After The Curve YouTube channel.
