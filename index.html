After The Curve stats

After The Curve stats is a small private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel After The Curve.

Once a week it reads that channel's own YouTube Analytics numbers (views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention and subscriber counts) and saves a short report on the owner's own computer. The owner uses the report to decide what to make next.

The tool is not offered to the public. It only ever signs in to the channel owner's own Google account, and it only reads data. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.
